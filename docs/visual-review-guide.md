# Visual Review Guide

Use this guide to review banners, landing pages, campaign pages, and AI-generated images.

## Hierarchy

- Can the viewer identify the main message first?
- Is the CTA visible without competing with the headline?
- Are secondary details clearly secondary?

## Composition

- Is the focal point intentional?
- Does spacing help scanning?
- Are crops safe for required formats?
- Does the design still work when scaled down?

## Brand Fit

- Are colors, type, imagery, and tone aligned with the brand?
- Does the design feel appropriate for the channel?
- Are visual effects serving the message?

## Risk Check

- Are product details accurate?
- Are claims, dates, and prices correct?
- Are AI-generated details visibly flawed?
- Could the visual create a misleading expectation?
