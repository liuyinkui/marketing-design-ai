# Brand Adaptation

Use this guide when adapting the workflow to a new brand.

## Gather

- Brand positioning
- Tone of voice
- Logo and usage rules
- Color and typography system
- Photography or illustration style
- Past high-performing examples
- Prohibited claims, visuals, or language

## Translate

- Map brand personality to copy tone.
- Map visual identity to layout, color, typography, and imagery.
- Identify which parts of the workflow need brand-specific rules.
- Document reusable decisions in `docs/` or the relevant skill file.

## Review

- Does the work look like it belongs to the brand?
- Does the tone match the audience and channel?
- Are mandatory brand elements present?
- Are any off-brand shortcuts being used because they are convenient?
