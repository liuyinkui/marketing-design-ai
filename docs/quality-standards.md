# Quality Standards

Use these standards before considering a marketing design deliverable ready.

## Strategy

- The objective is specific and measurable.
- The target audience is clear.
- The offer or value proposition is easy to understand.
- The deliverable has one primary job.

## Copy

- The headline communicates a benefit or clear message.
- The CTA describes a real action.
- Claims are accurate and supportable.
- Copy length fits the format.

## Visual

- The focal point is obvious.
- The hierarchy supports fast scanning.
- Brand elements feel intentional.
- Images do not misrepresent the product or offer.

## Production

- Required formats are complete.
- Text remains readable at final size.
- Links, dates, prices, and legal details are correct.
- Files are named clearly and stored in the right place.

## Ready Criteria

A deliverable is ready when it can be understood by the intended audience without additional explanation.
