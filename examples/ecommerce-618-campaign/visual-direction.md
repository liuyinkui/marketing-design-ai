# Visual Direction

## Concept

Summer Utility Sale

## Strategic Intent

Position the promotion as practical seasonal improvement, not only a discount event.

## Layout

- Product scene or grouped product focal point
- Bold headline in the top or left visual field
- Discount badge close to the product area
- CTA in a consistent high-contrast position
- Terms placed in smaller but readable text

## Image Direction

Bright natural light, clean surfaces, practical home and lifestyle objects, warm seasonal accents, and a sense of everyday usefulness.

## Color And Type

Use fresh warm accents with enough contrast for promotional text. Typography should feel clear, confident, and easy to scan on mobile.

## Production Notes

Create variants for desktop hero, mobile banner, square social ad, and email header. Test the headline and discount badge at the smallest required size.
