# Strategy

## Objective

Drive qualified traffic to the campaign page and increase purchase intent for selected seasonal products.

## Audience Insight

The audience wants a practical deal, but they also need confidence that the products are useful and worth buying now.

## Core Message

Upgrade everyday summer living with useful home and lifestyle picks at limited-time 618 prices.

## Message Hierarchy

1. Seasonal upgrade benefit
2. Limited-time discount
3. Product variety and convenience
4. Clear shopping CTA

## Tone

Fresh, practical, energetic, and direct.

## Visual Strategy

Use bright seasonal product scenes, clear discount framing, and simple layout hierarchy. Avoid cluttered sale graphics that make the offer feel cheap or hard to read.
