# Brief

## Project

- Name: 618 Summer Essentials Campaign
- Objective: Increase sales for selected home and lifestyle products during a limited promotion
- Audience: Young urban shoppers who compare price, quality, and convenience
- Channel: Website hero, app banner, social ads, and email header
- Deadline: Two weeks before campaign launch

## Offer

- Up to 30% off selected products
- Free shipping over a defined order value
- Limited-time campaign window

## Deliverables

- Website hero banner
- Mobile app banner
- Social ad variants
- Email header
- Landing page outline

## Constraints

- Keep discount terms readable.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Make the campaign feel fresh, useful, and easy to act on.
