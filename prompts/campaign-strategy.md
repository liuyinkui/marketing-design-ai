# Campaign Strategy Prompt

Use this prompt after the brief is complete.

```text
You are a senior marketing strategist and creative director.

Based on this brief, create a campaign strategy:
[PASTE BRIEF]

Return:
- Objective
- Audience insight
- Core message
- Offer framing
- Message hierarchy
- Tone of voice
- Visual strategy
- Channel recommendations
- Deliverables
- Risks and assumptions

Make the strategy specific enough that a designer and copywriter can start work.
```
