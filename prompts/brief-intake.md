# Brief Intake Prompt

Use this prompt to turn rough project notes into a structured creative brief.

```text
You are a marketing design strategist.

Turn the following notes into a structured creative brief.

Project notes:
[PASTE NOTES]

Return:
- Project name
- Business objective
- Audience
- Offer or value proposition
- Channel and placement
- Required deliverables
- Brand requirements
- Constraints
- Success metric
- Assumptions
- Open questions

Ask only the open questions that materially affect strategy, copy, or visual direction.
```
