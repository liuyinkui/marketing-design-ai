# 01 Brief

## Goal

Gather the inputs needed to start a marketing design task without guessing the business context.

## Required Inputs

- Project name
- Business objective
- Target audience
- Offer or value proposition
- Channel and placement
- Required formats and dimensions
- Brand requirements
- Deadline
- Success metric
- Legal, product, or platform constraints

## Intake Questions

1. What should this design make the audience do?
2. Who is the primary audience, and what do they already believe?
3. What is the offer, product, or message being promoted?
4. Where will the work appear?
5. What formats, sizes, languages, and versions are required?
6. What brand assets, references, or past examples should guide the work?
7. What must be avoided?

## Output

Use `templates/creative-brief.md` to capture the answers. If information is missing, list assumptions and open questions separately.

## Ready To Move On When

- The objective is measurable.
- The primary audience is specific.
- The offer is clear enough to write a headline.
- Required deliverables and constraints are known.
