# 03 Visual

## Goal

Create a visual direction that makes the message easy to understand and the action easy to take.

## Steps

1. Choose the visual concept based on the strategy.
2. Define layout hierarchy: focal point, headline, proof, CTA, supporting details.
3. Select image direction, product treatment, iconography, and background style.
4. Define typography, color, spacing, and responsive behavior.
5. Prepare asset requirements for AI image generation, design tools, or production.
6. Create at least two direction options when the brief allows exploration.

## Visual Direction Output

- Concept name
- Strategic intent
- Layout structure
- Image direction
- Color and typography notes
- Motion or interaction notes, if relevant
- Required assets
- Format-specific adaptations

## Quality Checks

- The focal point is clear.
- The CTA is visible and not crowded.
- The hierarchy still works on small screens.
- The visual tone fits the brand and audience.
- The image or illustration does not create false product expectations.
