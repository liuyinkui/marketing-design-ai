# 04 Review

## Goal

Check strategy, copy, visuals, and production details before delivery.

## Steps

1. Review the work against the original brief.
2. Check message clarity and CTA strength.
3. Verify brand alignment and visual consistency.
4. Test readability across required formats.
5. Confirm claims, pricing, dates, and legal details.
6. Check exports, links, filenames, and handoff notes.

## Review Checklist

- Objective is visible in the output.
- Audience benefit is clear.
- CTA is specific and easy to find.
- Visual hierarchy supports scanning.
- Copy is concise and accurate.
- Brand tone and assets are used correctly.
- Required formats are complete.
- Risks or assumptions are documented.

## Review Output

Use `prompts/review-checklist.md` when asking an AI agent to review a deliverable.
