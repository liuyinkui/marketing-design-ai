# Banner Design

## Purpose

Create clear, high-impact banners for paid ads, social posts, website placements, and campaign modules.

## Use When

- The output must communicate one message quickly.
- The format has limited space.
- The goal is click-through, awareness, announcement, or retargeting.

## Required Inputs

- Objective
- Audience
- Placement and dimensions
- Offer or message
- Brand assets
- CTA
- Any mandatory legal or product details

## Process

1. Define the single most important message.
2. Choose a hierarchy: product-first, offer-first, benefit-first, or urgency-first.
3. Create a layout with one focal point, one headline, and one CTA.
4. Adapt copy length to the smallest required size.
5. Check crop safety, readability, and brand consistency.

## Output Format

- Concept name
- Headline
- Supporting copy
- CTA
- Visual direction
- Layout notes
- Size adaptations
- Review risks

## Quality Checklist

- Define the goal and audience.
- Keep the message short and readable.
- Use strong visual hierarchy.
- Match the brand tone and palette.
- Keep the CTA visible without competing with the headline.
- Avoid adding secondary messages that dilute the banner.
