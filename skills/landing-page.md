# Landing Page

## Purpose

Create conversion-oriented landing pages that make one action feel obvious, valuable, and low-friction.

## Use When

- The page has one primary conversion goal.
- The audience arrives from a specific campaign, ad, email, or search intent.
- The page must persuade, qualify, or collect leads.

## Required Inputs

- Conversion goal
- Audience intent
- Main promise
- Offer or lead magnet
- Proof points
- Form or CTA requirements
- Analytics or tracking needs

## Recommended Structure

1. Hero with direct promise and CTA.
2. Problem and benefit framing.
3. Feature or offer details.
4. Proof and risk reduction.
5. FAQ or objection handling.
6. Final CTA.

## Output Format

- Page strategy
- Section-by-section outline
- Headline and CTA copy
- Visual hierarchy notes
- Form or conversion notes
- Review checklist

## Quality Checklist

- Start with a strong headline.
- Explain value quickly.
- Reduce friction in the layout.
- End with a visible CTA.
- Keep navigation minimal when conversion focus matters.
- Address the biggest objection before the final CTA.
