# Campaign Page

## Purpose

Create campaign-focused pages that explain an offer, build confidence, and move users toward a defined action.

## Use When

- A campaign needs a dedicated destination page.
- The page must support paid traffic, email traffic, social traffic, or QR-code traffic.
- The offer needs more context than a banner or ad can provide.

## Required Inputs

- Campaign objective
- Audience segment
- Offer and deadline
- Traffic source
- Product or service details
- Proof points
- Required CTA

## Page Structure

1. Hero: headline, value proposition, CTA, and campaign signal.
2. Offer detail: what users get and why it matters.
3. Proof: product benefits, testimonials, numbers, or trust signals.
4. How it works: steps, eligibility, or conditions.
5. Final CTA: concise next action.

## Output Format

- Page objective
- Section outline
- Headline options
- CTA options
- Visual direction
- Content requirements
- Measurement notes

## Quality Checklist

- Clarify the campaign objective.
- Present the core offer early.
- Add social proof or supporting proof points.
- Include a clear call to action.
- Keep campaign terms visible but not dominant.
- Make the page usable on mobile first.
