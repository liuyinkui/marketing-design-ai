# AI Image

## Purpose

Guide AI image generation and image selection for marketing work while protecting brand trust and production quality.

## Use When

- A campaign needs original imagery, moodboards, product scenes, or visual concepts.
- Existing assets are incomplete or unsuitable.
- The team needs prompt-ready image direction.

## Required Inputs

- Subject
- Brand style
- Channel and dimensions
- Mood or emotion
- Product accuracy requirements
- Restrictions, exclusions, and legal concerns

## Prompt Structure

- Subject and scene
- Composition and camera angle
- Lighting and color
- Style references
- Format and aspect ratio
- Negative constraints

## Output Format

- Image concept
- Generation prompt
- Negative prompt
- Aspect ratio
- Usage notes
- Review risks

## Quality Checklist

- Define style, subject, and format.
- Match the output to the channel.
- Check resolution and crop safety.
- Avoid distracting visual clutter.
- Do not imply product features that are not real.
- Review faces, hands, text, logos, and regulated claims carefully.
